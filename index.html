Chess Helper

Films your board with the back camera and draws Stockfish’s best move on the live picture.

  1. Set up the starting position. Put the phone on a stand on your side of the board, looking down at it.
  2. Tap the 4 corners of the board, starting under White’s left rook.
  3. Pick your colour and play. If a move is read wrong, tap Fix move; if the phone moved, tap Redo corners.

Use it for practice, analysis or with people who know. Against an opponent who doesn’t know, it’s cheating.
